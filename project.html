Learning from collaboration

What project team collaboration added to my development.

Working in a team helped me understand that different people notice different issues in a dataset or dashboard. This improved my willingness to listen, revise and explain my choices more clearly.

I learned to explain visuals in a way that others could understand quickly. This is directly relevant to a Data Management Analyst role where insights must be useful across marketing, product, data and frontline teams.

The target role requires cross-functional work. Team collaboration helped me practise balancing technical accuracy with stakeholder understanding and practical recommendations.
