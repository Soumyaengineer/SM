Target job alignment

CommSec Data Management Analyst: data, customer engagement and practical action.

The role asks for someone who can analyse data, improve data quality, support reporting and translate insight into frontline recommendations. That is the professional direction I am building towards.

My interpretation

The role goes beyond dashboards.

The strongest message in the advertisement is that data must create practical business value. I read this as a role that needs reporting capability, data quality awareness, stakeholder engagement and the ability to turn analysis into recommendations that frontline teams can use.
